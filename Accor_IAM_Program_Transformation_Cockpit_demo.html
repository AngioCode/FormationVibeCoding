Programme drift and loss of control		Multiple streams and dependencies may create delivery drift if the programme is not managed through a single control tower.High
Schedule slippage, unclear ownership, reduced visibility for executives.		PMO / Programme Director
  • Run weekly programme follow-up with stream owners.
  • Track risks, actions, dependencies and decisions.
  • Expose programme health in the Executive Command Center.
Mitigation in progress

Application onboarding industrialisation challenge		The onboarding factory may not scale if application owners, prerequisites and prioritisation are not ready early enough.Medium
Lower onboarding throughput, delayed coverage of critical applications, dependency on app owner availability.		Onboarding Factory Lead
  • Define onboarding readiness criteria.
  • Monitor backlog, discovery, build, testing and production stages.
  • Identify blocked applications and owners early.
Under watch
R5
Low adoption across Corporate & Hotels		Decentralised organisation and local practices may limit adoption of new IAM processes by managers, app owners and hotel populations.Medium
Limited usage of self-service journeys, persistent support load, reduced business value perception.		Change & Adoption Lead

  • Activate IAM Academy, webinars and office hours.
  • Use app owner onboarding as adoption accelerator.
Mitigation in progress

Delayed compliance evidence, audit findings not closed, lower risk reduction impact.		Compliance / Security Lead

  • Review compliance checkpoints after each go-live.
